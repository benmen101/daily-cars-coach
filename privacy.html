Privacy Policy

Last updated June 2, 2026

Daily CARS Coach is designed to collect as little as possible. We do not run our own servers, we do not have user accounts, and we do not sell or share your personal data. This policy explains exactly what happens with your information.

Information stored on your device

Your practice history, scores, streaks, and settings are stored locally on your device only. This information is not transmitted to us and is removed when you delete the app.

Purchases

If you subscribe to Pro, the purchase is processed by Apple and managed through RevenueCat, our subscription provider. RevenueCat assigns a random, anonymous identifier to your app installation to determine whether your subscription is active. We never receive your name, payment details, or Apple ID. See Apple's Privacy Policy and RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

Content reports

If you tap “Report” on a passage or question, the app opens your own email app with a prefilled message (the item's ID and your note) addressed to us. Nothing is sent unless you choose to send the email, and any information we receive that way comes directly from your email, not the app.

Analytics & tracking

The app does not include third-party advertising or cross-app tracking. We do not use the Advertising Identifier (IDFA).

Children's privacy

The app is intended for students preparing for graduate-level admissions exams and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
