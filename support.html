Frequently asked

How do I report a bad passage or question?

Tap the Report button on any passage or question inside the app. It opens your mail app prefilled with the details so we can review and fix it. The item is also hidden from your account immediately.

How do subscriptions work?

The free tier includes one passage every day. Pro unlocks unlimited passages, timed full-section simulations, and reasoning-type analytics. Subscriptions are billed through your Apple ID and renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel any time in Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device.

How do I restore a purchase?

Open the app's paywall and tap Restore Purchases, or reinstall the app while signed in to the same Apple ID.

Is my data shared?

No. Your scores, streaks, and history stay on your device. See our Privacy Policy for details.
